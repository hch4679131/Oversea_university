UNIVERSITY PARTNERS

University Partners

AUTHORIZATION

Jesselton University College (JUC)

SDLV partners with Jesselton University College (JUC) as an officially recognized recruitment partner, actively supporting student recruitment and program development. JUC is a leading private higher education institution in Sabah, focused on flexible and interdisciplinary education. JUC is an approved learning center designated by OTHM Qualification (UK). Over the years of global expansion, JUC became an institutional member of ACBSP, AHEA, and CACE. JUC also signed an academic collaboration agreement with The Hang Seng University of Hong Kong (HSUHK) to promote excellence in teaching and research.

AUTHORIZATION

Technological and Higher Education Institute of Hong Kong (THEi)

SDLV is an authorized partner of THEi in Mainland China and overseas, responsible for consultation, promotion, and student referrals for its degree programs. THEi is a member institution of the Vocational Training Council (VTC) and offers 20+ practice-oriented bachelor’s and master’s degree programs across high-growth industries in Hong Kong and the Asia-Pacific region.
