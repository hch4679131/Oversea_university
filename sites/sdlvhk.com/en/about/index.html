About Us

We Are Your Family's
"Strongest Support"

SDLV is a one-stop student growth support brand based in Hong Kong and facing the world. Our core team consists of HK locals and global experts.

From academics to housing, career to life, we provide reliable, full-chain, and warm companionship, empowering every step of your journey.

Mission

To be the most steadfast support on every student's growth journey

Vision

Guided by experience and expertise, empowering students to unlock their potential

Our History

Milestones
2016
First Step | Founded in Hong Kong

Inspired by the founder's own study-abroad challenges in Australia, we set out to build a service that truly understands students. We opened our first office in Hong Kong, starting with UK and Australia admissions consulting.

2017
Team Growth | Expanded Destinations

We brought together experts with Hong Kong/Macau and overseas study backgrounds, expanded HK/Macau admissions services, and established our full-cycle companionship model.

2019
Housing Plan | Beyond Academics

To solve the “hard-to-find housing” pain point, we launched our student housing brand and built a safe, community-driven living experience—evolving from academic advisors to life partners.

2020
Resilience | Service Upgrade

We integrated education, housing, and career services. During the pandemic, we strengthened online support and served more than 20,000 students.

2022
Mainland Expansion | Recognition

We opened our first mainland branch in Guiyang to serve students in Southwest China, and received Tencent News Education's 2022 Influential Study Abroad Service Brand award.

2023
Nationwide Network | Strategy in Place

We expanded into Foshan, Wuhan, and Shenzhen, building a collaborative network centered in Hong Kong with four mainland hubs—upgrading into a nationwide one-stop growth support platform.

2024
Award-Winning | Brand Elevation

We joined the Guangdong International Education Promotion Association and earned Sina & Weibo's 2024 Comprehensive Strength Study Abroad Service Brand recognition.

2026
Ecosystem Launch | Scaled Impact

We surpassed 50,000 students served. Through systematic enablement, we are building a nationwide support ecosystem—bringing our mission of empowering students to life across China.
